PomNate! Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

PomNate! (package com.pomnate.game) is developed by Sutejo Kurniawan ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. PomNate! does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or device data. The game works fully offline. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Game settings (such as sound, animation quality and match options) are saved only on your device so they persist between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Children

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. Google Play may collect data under Google's own privacy policy when you download the app.

Changes

If future versions add features such as online multiplayer, we will update this policy before release and change the effective date above.
